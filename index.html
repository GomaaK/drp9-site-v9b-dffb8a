11 / questions

Before you message us.

What does DRP9 Tech build?

DRP9 Tech builds mobile apps, web platforms and marketplaces, AI agents and automation, and runs dedicated product teams that build and operate a product with you.

Can you build an app for my project?

Yes. Tell us what you are building, the stage it is at and your timeline. We reply by email and agree scope before any work starts.

Who is behind DRP9 Tech?

DRP9 Tech is led by three co-founders: Khaled Gomaa (product and marketplaces), Kareem Zaher (engineering leadership) and Mohamed El Sawaf (brand and growth). Between them they have led product and engineering at BetterUp, Booking.com, Catawiki, 4Sale, elmenus and Cartona.

Where are you based?

We work from Cairo, Amsterdam, Lisbon and Dubai, with sister companies in the UAE and Portugal that share owners, partners, teams and projects.

Which companies are part of the ecosystem?

Rise Personal (personal branding, Dubai), Bnsights (innovation and government excellence consulting, Dubai), Imolty.ai and Uniek AI (AI products, Lisbon).
